  willing to name you, that carries further than a cold application. Ask them first.

  full-cycle implementation. Applying without that is the kind of thing an HR manager remembers
  when your name comes up for a role you do fit.

  consider my experience closer to the Senior role, I would be glad to be assessed for it."*
