Tools I Built for My School

  • Grading and report cards (SAIS Portal, in production) — teachers enter marks; averages count only the subjects a pupil actually took, so a mid-year transfer is not penalised; report cards reach parents over WhatsApp.
  • Timetabling (EduScheduler Pro) — builds a full timetable for 20 classes, 63 teachers and 615 lessons a week, with no clashes, in under three minutes — work that had taken the school weeks by hand.
  • Lesson-plan review (Academic Compliance, in use) — checks teachers’ weekly lesson plans against Cambridge planning standards across ten departments and routes them to the right head of department.
  • Report-card comments — an AI-assisted comment writer running in 13 class spreadsheets.
  • Learning-gap diagnostics (BaseCamp, pilot) — finds the specific misconception behind a pupil’s wrong answer and suggests a targeted lesson; entered in the UNICEF StartUp Lab Learning Gaps Challenge, 2026.
